## 📲 Quick Installation

Add all **27 plugins** to your Nuvio app in under a minute:

1. Open **Nuvio** on your device.
2. Navigate to **Settings** → **Plugins** → **Add new repository**.
3. Paste the manifest URL below:

```text
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hihihihihiiray/nuvio-plugins/refs/heads/main/manifest.json
```

4. Tap **Add** and toggle your preferred providers on or off.
